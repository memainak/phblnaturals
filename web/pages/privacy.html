Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]. Have a lawyer review this before you go live.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what rights you have under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Order details: name, phone, email, delivery address, order contents.
  • Payment details: handled entirely by our payment gateway, Razorpay. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials.
  • Support messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, email or the contact form.
  • Site usage: basic analytics, and items you keep in your cart or wishlist, which are stored in your own browser.

Why we use it

To process and deliver your order, confirm COD orders, answer your questions, handle returns and refunds, meet tax and legal obligations, and, if you opt in, send offers. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to fulfil your order: our courier partners, our payment gateway, our WhatsApp and email providers, and our accountants and tax authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept as long as tax law requires. Marketing contacts are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Write to our Grievance Officer and we respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We use only what the site needs to work, plus analytics if you accept it. Your cart and wishlist live in your browser's local storage, never on our servers.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 18.
