Returns & refunds

Last updated: [date]. Have a lawyer review this before you go live.

Return window

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

What can be returned

  • Unopened products with the seal intact.
  • Products that arrived damaged, leaking or wrong. Send photos within 48 hours of delivery.

What cannot be returned

  • Opened or used products, for hygiene reasons, unless they were faulty on arrival.
  • Free gifts and items marked non-returnable.
  • Products damaged by misuse or storage in direct heat.

How to start a return

Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number and photos. We arrange a reverse pickup where the courier services your pincode; otherwise we ask you to self-ship and we reimburse the actual postage.

Refunds

Once we receive and check the product, prepaid refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. COD refunds go to a UPI ID or bank account you share with us. Delivery charges are not refunded unless the error was ours.

Cancellations

You can cancel any order before it is dispatched for a full refund. Once dispatched, treat it as a return.
