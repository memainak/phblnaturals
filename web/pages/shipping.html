Shipping policy

Last updated: [date]. Have a lawyer review this before you go live.

Where we ship

We ship across India. We do not ship internationally yet.

Charges

Delivery is free on prepaid and COD orders of ₹499 and above. Below that, a flat ₹59 delivery charge applies and is shown at checkout before you pay.

Dispatch and delivery time

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are usually dispatched the same or next working day. Delivery normally takes 3 to 7 working days depending on your pincode. Remote pincodes can take longer.

Tracking

You receive a tracking link by email and WhatsApp once the parcel is picked up. You can also use our Track Order page.

Cash on Delivery

COD is available on most pincodes. We confirm COD orders on WhatsApp before dispatch; unconfirmed orders may be cancelled.

Failed or refused deliveries

Couriers attempt delivery up to three times. Parcels refused or undelivered after that return to us. For prepaid orders we refund the amount minus the actual shipping cost. Repeated refusals may mean we restrict COD for that number.
