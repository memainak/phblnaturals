How we formulate

Every PHBL Naturals formula starts from a traditional ingredient the lab already knows well: neem, tulsi, bhringraj, turmeric, aloe. We pair it with modern cosmetic science so the texture, stability and shelf life meet what you expect from a product you use every day.

We make cosmetic claims only. Our products cleanse, nourish, moisturise and reduce the appearance of concerns such as dullness or dryness. They are not medicines and we do not claim they treat or cure anything.

What we promise

  • Made in our own lab. Not outsourced to a third-party manufacturer.
  • Honest labels. Full ingredient lists, net quantity, MRP inclusive of taxes, and our address on every pack.
  • No animal testing. We do not test our cosmetic products on animals.
  • Real support. A person on WhatsApp, not a bot.
